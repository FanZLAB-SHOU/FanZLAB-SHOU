Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; their inclusion identifies tools used by the laboratory and does not imply endorsement.

Source paths: `icons/r/r-original.svg`, `icons/python/python-original.svg`, `icons/cplusplus/cplusplus-original.svg`, `icons/git/git-original.svg`, `icons/github/github-original.svg` at the Devicon repository's `master` branch. Retrieved 2026-10-17.

The GitHub silhouette is rendered light against the dark panel; other glyph colours are unchanged. TMB is identified by its name rather than an invented brand logo.
